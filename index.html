Capabilities

Built for Enforcement Officers

Every feature maps to a real step in the Legal Metrology inspection workflow — from field photography to the final audit trail.

📸

Camera & OCR Scanning

Point your camera at a product label or barcode. Tesseract OCR reads the text; the barcode scanner decodes EAN-13, QR, and more.

⚖️

Compliance Rule Engine

Six mandatory declaration checks run automatically — product name, manufacturer, net quantity, MRP, packing date, consumer care.

📊

Live Dashboard

Real-time analytics, violation breakdowns, filterable audit logs, and exportable compliance reports for every inspection.

The Pipeline

From photo to verdict, ten steps

Every uploaded label moves down the same conveyor — each stage hands off exactly one job to the next, so a violation can always be traced back to the step that caught it.

01

Officer uploads the product image

Controller

A field officer photographs the packet label and submits it through the Spring Boot upload endpoint. Try it on the Upload & Scan page.

02

Image preprocessing

OpenCV

Deskew, denoise, and contrast-correct the photo so a crumpled or glare-lit label still reads cleanly.

03

OCR text extraction

Tesseract

The cleaned image is converted into raw text — manufacturer, quantity, price, dates, all in one block.

04

Declaration field extraction

Regex

Pattern matching pulls the mandatory fields out of the raw OCR block into structured values.

05

Compliance rule engine

ComplianceRules.java

Each extracted field is checked against the Legal Metrology declaration requirements.

06

Verdict stamped

Compliant / Non-compliant

Every missing or malformed field is logged as a violation and the product is stamped with a final verdict.

07

Inspection record stored

MySQL

Product details, extracted fields and violations are written to the inspection log.

08

Compliance report generated

JasperReports

A printable report is produced for the case file — see a sample report.

09

Enforcement dashboard updates

Live totals

Aggregate counts refresh on the dashboard so a team can see the day's load at a glance.

10

Case closed — filed for audit

Archive

The image, extracted text, verdict and report are retained together for later re-verification.
